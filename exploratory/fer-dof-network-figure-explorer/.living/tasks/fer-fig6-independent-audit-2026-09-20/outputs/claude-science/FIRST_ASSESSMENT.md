## Verdicts on the six questions

**Q1 — endodermis edge/score mismatch: confirmed issue, real, correctly compared.**
From the public exports alone: in exactly three genotype-contexts (Maturation, Proliferation-Domain and Transition-Domain endodermis, fer-4 only) the out-degree implied by the exported 20,000-edge lists disagrees with the saved `degree_out`; the other 84 genotype-contexts agree gene-for-gene on both out- and in-degree, and no exported edge list contains duplicate pairs. My counts reconcile exactly with the frozen remote JSON once its definitions are applied (e.g. Maturation fer-4: 668 common-gene mismatches + 103 scored-but-edgeless + 6 zero-degree-scored-with-edges = 777; 231 − 6 = 225). The comparison stage is right: CellOracle's `merged_score` is defined from `filtered_links`, and the run script filters (`p=0.01, coef_abs, top 20,000`) then scores in sequence. Two new observations: (i) `degree_out` sums to exactly 20,000 in the three bad clusters too, so the scores describe *some* 20k-edge graph, not a pre-filter graph; (ii) the score node set differs substantially from the edge node set (462–623 scored nodes absent from edges; 671–1,013 edge nodes absent from scores; out-degree correlation 0.44), which rules out a top-20k tie-break artefact and points to two different fits or two different filter runs being saved together. Which side is stale cannot be decided from local data; it requires re-filtering the unfiltered `endodermis_rawRNA.links.celloracle.links` on Resnick and comparing to both components. A read-only script for that is provided (`checks/remote_endodermis_refilter_check.py`, not executed here).

**Exposure (new, quantified).** The three inconsistent contexts contribute 325 of 1,105 TF nomination rows (29%); 78 of the 512 GRN-TFs are nominated *only* there; removing them would change the classifiable foregrounds from 19→17 DOF (WT) and 23→22 (fer-4); 23 of the 96 DOF context nominations and 11 of the 12 endodermal nominations among the ten assayed DOFs (7 of 8 populated endodermis tiles in panel c) come from them. They are also the three highest-yield contexts in the whole ranking (103, 108, 114 nominations vs. a next-highest of 82 and a median of ~16) — either genuine endodermal rewiring or an artefact of comparing WT scores with fer-4 scores from a different fit. This is unknown effect, not established error, but the coincidence should be stated wherever the endodermis column or DOF4.6/DAG1 endodermal nominations are shown.

**Q2 — phloem/xylem pole-pericycle admission: confirmed, gene-feature level, counts exact.**
The public run script selects cells with `contains("phloem") & ~contains("pericycle")` (correct) but selects DE features with `cluster_id.str.contains(x)` alone, so "Phloem/Xylem Pole Pericycle" DE genes enter the phloem/xylem target features. Reconstructed from `sig_de_fil.csv`, the Kay TF list and the saved H5AD universes: 349 of 3,359 phloem and 650 of 3,635 xylem input genes are admitted only via pole-pericycle contexts — the stated numbers exactly. Every tissue's saved universe equals TF ∪ substring-DE ∩ adata.var, so the rule is confirmed, not inferred. YUC5 survives a clean phloem universe but drops from xylem. Consequence: contaminated targets occupy some of the 20k retained edges and feed degree scores; post-hoc edge deletion is not equivalent to a refit (imputation, TF candidate set and the top-20k cut all depend on the gene set), though per-target ridge coefficients themselves are fitted independently.
