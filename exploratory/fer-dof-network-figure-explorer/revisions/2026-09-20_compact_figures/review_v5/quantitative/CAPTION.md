# TF-family enrichment among network-nominated regulators

The same ten families appear in both genotype panels, ranked by the larger WT-associated or fer-4-associated odds ratio among families with at least ten eligible members. Points show conditional maximum-likelihood odds ratios and two-sided 95% Fisher confidence intervals on a shared logarithmic scale. Filled and open points denote BH-adjusted P < 0.05 and P >= 0.05, respectively, using one-sided enrichment P values and retaining the original correction across all 55 families within each genotype. **Nominated / eligible** reports distinct nominated TF family members divided by eligible members of that family in the 1,292-TF paired-score universe. For example, 19/33 means 19 nominated DOF members among 33 eligible DOF members. These counts are not replicates. The left-pointing arrow marks the WT WOX estimate of zero, which cannot be positioned on a logarithmic axis. Enrichments are conditional on the regulatory prior and do not directly test differences between genotypes.

The optional four-family version shows the same estimates for families significant in at least one genotype.

These figures remain exploratory candidates because the prior source-analysis limitations persist: saved edges and degree scores are inconsistent in three fer-4 endodermal contexts, and phloem/xylem fits have separate target-mapping issues. This revision changes only the labels and layout; it does not resolve those analyses.

# Cell-type nominations among the ten assayed DOFs

Upper and lower tile halves denote WT-associated and fer-4-associated nominations, respectively. **Nominated / scored zones** reports the number of nominated developmental zones divided by the number of zones scored in both genotypes for that gene and cell type. For example, 1/4 denotes nomination in one of four zones with paired genotype scores. These are context counts, not biological replicate counts. A grey half has paired scores without nomination; an x denotes no paired-score zone. All 130 cells currently displayed have at least one paired-score zone, so no x occurs in this panel. Both coloured halves indicate opposite nomination directions in different zones. The source top-100 and pooled-tail selection rule and relative out-degree ranking are unchanged. Complete missingness counts remain in the source table. The inherited analysis limitations above also apply here.
