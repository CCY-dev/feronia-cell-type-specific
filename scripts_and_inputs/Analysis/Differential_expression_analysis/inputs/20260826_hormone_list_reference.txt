Every attribution below marked [VERIFIED] was checked GENE-FOR-GENE against the
paper's own supplementary tables, not merely by matching gene counts.

1. Nemhauser JL, Hong F, Chory J (2006)                              [VERIFIED]
   "Different plant hormones regulate similar processes through largely
    nonoverlapping transcriptional responses."
   Cell 126(3):467-475.  DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2006.05.050   PMID 16901781

   Covers FIVE hormones -- ABA, ACC, CK, GA, IAA:

     file          genes   = UP  + DN    Nemhauser table
     ABA.csv        2914   = 1438 + 1476   Table S1 (mmc2)
     ACC.csv         532   =  167 +  365   Table S2 (mmc3)
     GA.csv          122   =   40 +   82   Table S4 (mmc5)
     IAA.csv         785   =  430 +  355   Table S5 (mmc6)
     CK.csv          495   =  332 +  163   Table S7 (mmc8)

   The matching X_UP.csv / X_DN.csv files are identical to that table's "up" and
   "down" columns respectively.

   CONSTRUCTION RULE: X.csv = X_UP union X_DN, and EXCLUDES Nemhauser's small
   "complex" category (genes up at some timepoints and down at others: ABA 20,
   ACC 8, GA 3, IAA 6, CK 3). So X.csv is the linear-model p<0.01 set with
   complex dropped -- e.g. ABA 2914, not 2934.

   NOTE ON NAMING: "ACC" and "IAA" are Nemhauser's own treatment names (the
   ethylene precursor, and indole-3-acetic acid). Do not read ACC.csv as a
   general ethylene list -- see Zhang_2018_ET.csv below.

2. Pajerowska-Mukhtar KM, Wang W, Tada Y, Oka N, Tucker CL, Fonseca JP,
   Dong X (2012)                                                     [VERIFIED]
   "The HSF-like Transcription Factor TBF1 Is a Major Molecular Switch for
    Plant Growth-to-Defense Transition."
   Curr Biol 22(2):103-112.  DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2011.12.015   PMID 22244999

   Covers SA:

     file          genes   = UP  + DN    source sheet in Table S1 (mmc2.xls)
     SA.csv         1051   =  518 +  533   "SA induced only" / "SA repressed only"

   *** IMPORTANT INTERPRETATION CAVEAT ***
   Those sheets are "induced/repressed ONLY". Genes co-regulated by the MAMP
   elf18 are held in SEPARATE sheets ("SA and elf18 induced" 37, "elf18 induced
   only" 475, "elf18 repressed only" 1092, etc). SA.csv therefore contains
   SA-SPECIFIC responsive genes -- it is NOT a complete SA-responsive gene set.
   This is why PR1 (AT2G14610) and PR2 (AT3G57260) are ABSENT from SA.csv: both
   sit in "elf18 repressed only". Do not describe this list as "SA-responsive"
   in a figure legend without that qualifier.

4. Nolan TM, Brennan B, Yang M, Chen J, Zhang M, Li Z, Wang X, Bassham DC,
   Walley J, Yin Y (2017)
   "Selective Autophagy of BES1 Mediated by DSK2 Balances Plant Growth and
    Survival."
   Dev Cell 41(1):33-46.e7.  DOI 10.1016/j.devcel.2017.03.013   PMID 28399398

   AND

   Wang X, Chen J, Xie Z, Liu S, Nolan T, Ye H, Zhang M, Guo H, Schnable PS,
   Li Z, Yin Y (2014)
   "Histone lysine methyltransferase SDG8 is involved in brassinosteroid-
    regulated gene expression in Arabidopsis thaliana."
   Mol Plant 7(8):1303-1315.  DOI 10.1093/mp/ssu056   PMID 24838002

   Cover BR (cite BOTH):

     file                genes
     BR.csv               4987
     BR_UP.csv            2678
     BR_DN.csv            2376
     BR_2K_list.csv       2160   = genes regulated in BOTH the RNA-seq and the
                                   microarray experiment (the INTERSECTION of
                                   the two papers), per README_hormone_reference.md

   *** NOT verified gene-for-gene *** -- the two papers' supplements were never
   compared directly. Supporting evidence only: BR.csv contains 98.6% of
   BR_2K_list.csv (2130/2160), so BR.csv is most likely the UNION of the two
   papers' BR-responsive genes while BR_2K_list is their intersection.
   BR.csv is definitely NOT from Nemhauser: it shares only 6.8% (341 genes) with
   Nemhauser's BL set (647 genes).
   Also note BR.csv != BR_UP union BR_DN (4987 vs 5054; 67 union genes are
   absent from BR.csv).

   JA_UP.csv (1760) and JA_DN.csv (2555)
     Source UNKNOWN. These are NOT Nemhauser's MJ set (only 19.9% / 7.8%
     overlap with MJ up/down), and NOT Hickman (union of 4315 vs 3611, 1159
     shared). They also carry 30 organellar (ATCG*/ATMG*) genes, which the
     2006 ATH1 array did not assay -- so they are from a later, RNA-seq-era
     experiment. There is no JA.csv. Use Hickman_2017_JA.csv for JA.

================================================================================
DO NOT CONFUSE: UNLABELLED vs LABELLED FILES
================================================================================

Files named <HORMONE>.csv and files named <Author>_<Year>_<HORMONE>.csv are
DIFFERENT DATASETS, not copies. They barely overlap:

   ABA.csv (2914)  vs  song_2016_ABA.csv (3061)   shared 1025   Jaccard 0.207
   ACC.csv (532)   vs  Zhang_2018_ET.csv (1292)   shared  140   Jaccard 0.083
   GA.csv  (122)   vs  Bai_2012_GA.csv   (3570)   shared   37   Jaccard 0.010

The labelled files are the "additional sets" described in
README_hormone_reference.md and belong to the papers named in their filenames:

   song_2016_ABA.csv   Song L, ..., Ecker JR (2016) Science 354(6312):aag1550
                       DOI 10.1126/science.aag1550
   Bai_2012_GA.csv     Bai MY, ..., Wang ZY (2012) Nat Cell Biol
                       DOI 10.1038/ncb2546
   Zhang_2018_ET.csv   Zhang F, Wang L, Ko EE, Shao K, Qiao H (2018) Plant Cell
                       DOI 10.1105/tpc.17.00671
   Hickman_2017_JA.csv Hickman R, ... (2017) Plant Cell  DOI 10.1105/tpc.16.00958

Picking the wrong one silently changes every downstream enrichment result.

================================================================================
NOTE ON "Guo et al 2018 Current Biology"
================================================================================

README_hormone_reference.md states that the unlabelled csvs are "the same as
those used in Guo et al 2018 Current Biology" -- Guo H, Nolan TM, Song G,
Liu S, Xie Z, Chen J, Schnable PS, Walley JW, Yin Y (2018) Curr Biol
28(20):3316-3324, DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2018.07.078.

That paper is the COMPILATION these lists were inherited through; it did not
generate them. Its own RNA-seq was the fer-4 transcriptome. Cite the PRIMARY
papers listed above in a methods section, not Guo et al. 2018.
